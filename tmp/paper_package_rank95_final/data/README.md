# V3 through V33 evaluation data

`game_history.txt` is the raw dashboard text copied after the V3 batch. `v3_batch_aggregate.csv` was derived from its 66 distinct dashboard summary entries. The raw text also contains selected detailed transcripts that overlap those summary entries, so transcript blocks were not counted as additional games.

The per-game rating changes displayed by the dashboard are rounded to one decimal place. Their sums can therefore differ slightly from the change between the higher-precision before and after rating snapshots. The batch is observational: configurations, roles, and opponents were not controlled or randomized between policy versions.

`v4_bargaining_games.csv` records the 25 V4 bargaining entries supplied from the dashboard, in displayed newest-to-oldest order. All entries were agreements against agents. `v4_bargaining_aggregate.csv` contains the overall and role-level counts and means. The rounded deltas sum to 46.2, while the two-decimal rating snapshots changed by 46.19.

The V4 summary does not include full configuration-level transcripts. It can support agreement, role, and displayed-rating descriptions, but it cannot identify which V4 mechanism caused the observed change.

`v5_negotiation_aggregate.csv` records the exact V5 negotiation snapshot: 1243.31 after 72 games before the run and 1344.87 after 127 games afterward, a 55-game change of +101.56. The notebook reported zero fallbacks and 46 named or game-local negotiation profiles.

`v5_negotiation_visible_games.csv` records all 55 supplied dashboard rows in newest-to-oldest order. They contain 36 agreements, nine no-deals, and ten walkaways; buyer and seller rounded deltas average +1.07 and +2.72, respectively. Their rounded sum (+101.6) differs from the exact snapshot change (+101.56) by 0.04 because displayed per-game deltas have only one decimal place.

`v6_persuasion_games.csv` records all 35 V6 persuasion entries in newest-to-oldest display order. `v6_persuasion_aggregate.csv` records the exact rating change from 1756.91 after 379 games to 1741.90 after 414 games (-15.01), with zero fallbacks. The displayed deltas sum to -15.0. Buyer games sum to +0.3 across 21 games (mean +0.01); seller games sum to -15.3 across 14 games (mean -1.09). The batch is a complete descriptive negative result, not a randomized comparison.

`v7_bargaining_games.csv` records all 40 V7 bargaining rows in newest-to-oldest display order. Every game reached agreement. `v7_bargaining_aggregate.csv` records the exact change from 1352.06 after 97 games to 1361.95 after 137 games (+9.89). Rounded deltas sum to +9.8. Alice sums to -9.9 across 27 games (mean -0.37), while Bob sums to +19.7 across 13 games (mean +1.52). The notebook harvested 40 terminal rewards. Its displayed diagnostic count of five arose from attempted reward lookups for synthetic offline-test IDs and is not a live-action fallback count.

`v8_negotiation_games.csv` records all 44 V8 negotiation rows in newest-to-oldest order, including one seller-versus-human game. `v8_negotiation_aggregate.csv` records the exact change from 1344.87 after 127 games to 1395.83 after 171 games (+50.96), plus role and outcome summaries. Rounded deltas sum to +50.9. Buyers sum to +10.7 across 15 games (mean +0.71); sellers sum to +40.2 across 29 games (mean +1.39). The run harvested all 44 terminal rewards with zero live action fallbacks and zero telemetry diagnostics.

`v10_negotiation_games.csv` records the two supplied V10 games, both buyer walkaways against agents, with rounded changes +2.9 and +0.2. `v10_negotiation_aggregate.csv` records the exact change from 1395.13 after 173 games to 1398.16 after 175 games (+3.03). The SDK completed two games after a one-game request, so V10 harvested two terminal diagnostics but correctly assigned no live rating credit and stopped on its overshoot guard. There were zero action fallbacks and zero telemetry diagnostics. The notebook's one displayed rating-attributed outcome is generated by its offline unit test and is not included as live evidence.

`v11_negotiation_games.csv` records all 53 V11 rows in newest-to-oldest order. `v11_negotiation_aggregate.csv` records the exact change from 1398.16 after 175 games to 1353.32 after 228 games (-44.84). Rounded deltas sum to -44.9. Buyers sum to -19.4 across 30 games (mean -0.65); sellers sum to -25.5 across 23 games (mean -1.11). Agreements sum to -26.6, no-deals to -23.9, and walkaways to +5.6. The run requested 24 games at concurrency four but completed 53, all on `v5_safe`, with 53 harvested terminal diagnostics, zero action fallbacks, and zero telemetry diagnostics. This is a negative result for the large-batch runner, not an identified change to the negotiation decision core.

V33's canonical artifacts are bundled under `../artifacts/v33/` with SHA-256 hashes. Excluding the single synthetic `rating-credit-live-like` row, `v33_rating_outcomes.csv` contains 248 live outcomes. Bargaining gained 190.94 over 150 games, persuasion gained 55.67 over 78, and negotiation lost 33.73 over 20. Alice gained 80.15 and Bob 110.79; persuasion buyers gained 34.77 and sellers 20.90; negotiation buyers lost 20.33 and sellers 13.40. They did not alter actions or ratings.
