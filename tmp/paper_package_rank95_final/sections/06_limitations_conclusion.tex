\section{Limitations and conclusion}

This is one post-selection, fixed-order competition trace. Matchmaking, role,
configuration, opponent, rating shrinkage, and time are uncontrolled; two
families are adaptively censored. Displayed rating deltas are platform measures,
not welfare, and the transcript export is incomplete and unlinked. Fixed text
also cannot test whether richer dialogue would help. Strategic persuasion can
transfer to manipulative settings; explicit labeling as a calibration heuristic
and restriction to the competition reduce but do not remove that concern.

The exact executed notebook and evidence are bundled with hashes in
\path{artifacts/v33/}; the analysis regenerates the statistical appendix tables,
derived CSVs, summary, and sequential figure from a clean extraction. Actions
present in the append-only evidence can be audited,
but exact ratings, missing transcript records, and unrecorded counterfactuals
cannot be replayed.

The case study shows why agent evaluation should separate strategic policy,
behavioral process measures, contract validity, and exposure control. Its
positive bargaining and persuasion totals and negative negotiation total are
observations about this run, not evidence that the selected mechanisms caused
those outcomes.
