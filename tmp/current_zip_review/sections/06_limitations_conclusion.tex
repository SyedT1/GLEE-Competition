\section{Limitations, reproducibility, and conclusion}

V33 is one sequential competition run, not a controlled policy comparison.
Matchmaking, configurations, roles, rating shrinkage, and opponent adaptation
are uncontrolled; adaptive stops make family sample sizes unequal. Displayed
rating deltas measure the competition system rather than raw welfare, and no
configuration-matched baseline or uncertainty interval is available. Fixed
messages may also underperform richer dialogue against humans or language
models. Strategic persuasion techniques could transfer to manipulative sales
settings; transparent bounds, audit logs, and restriction to the competition
environment mitigate but do not remove that concern.
The transcript export covers 220/248 rated assignments, omits game identifiers,
and contains two recoverable missing headers; behavioral counts therefore use
available cases and cannot be linked safely to individual rating changes.

The exact submitted implementation is
\path{notebooks/glee_competition_33/GLEE_Competition_agent_v33_heuristic_150_each.ipynb}
at \CodeURL. The same directory contains the session report, 249-row rating
CSV (one synthetic test plus 248 live rows), policy state, and append-only
evidence. The run used Python 3.12 and \texttt{glee-sdk} in a hosted Kaggle
session. The method required no training or GPU and took approximately 13,497
seconds (3.75 hours); the provider's exact CPU, memory, and accelerator
allocation was not recorded.
Actions are reproducible from saved states, but exact leaderboard ratings are
not because matchmaking and rating models change.

The central lesson is that reliable strategic agency has three coupled layers:
economic reasoning, behavioral alignment, and exposure control. V33's positive
bargaining and persuasion signs are observations, not estimates of the effects
of its role-local mechanisms; the negative negotiation sample further warns
against treating historical leaderboard evidence as stationary. Future
evaluation should interleave frozen role baselines within matched contexts and
preserve corrected validators, synthetic isolation, and monitoring guards.
