\section{Overview and motivation}

GLEE evaluates agents in repeated bargaining, bilateral trade, and persuasion
with natural-language interaction, heterogeneous horizons, and incomplete
information \citep{shapira2024glee}. An effective entrant must reason about
incentives and other players while always returning a legal action under a turn
deadline. Our goal was an auditable agent whose economic decisions do not
depend on opaque model inference or training.

The resulting V33 agent is fully heuristic and deterministic conditional on the
visible state. It separates policies by family and role; compresses dialogue
history into economically meaningful quantities such as demands, price steps,
stalls, and observed recommendation precision; and validates every action
before submission. A one-assignment controller checks authoritative rating and
game counts between matches so a strong role cannot silently subsidize an
unsafe one.

This paper contributes (i) a compact strategic design for all three GLEE
families, including a persuasion schedule bounded by a static receiver-obedience
benchmark; (ii) an agent
behavior analysis linking executable safeguards to observed deviations; and
(iii) evidence from 248 V33 games. The evidence is intentionally reported as a
sequential competition trace rather than a controlled superiority claim.
