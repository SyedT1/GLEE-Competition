\section{Sequential development variants and live trace}

The complete dashboard export contains 1,704 games. Summed one-decimal changes
were $+535.9$, $+695.0$, and $+833.0$ for bargaining, negotiation, and
persuasion. Their per-game mean/median/SD were respectively
$1.11/0.0/5.02$, $1.24/1.2/4.39$, and $1.26/0.9/4.57$; sign counts were
$240/3/240$, $363/0/199$, and $361/5/293$. Chronological-third gains
attenuated from $364.4$ to $-27.6$ to $199.1$, $422.8$ to $159.0$ to
$113.2$, and $508.1$ to $261.7$ to $63.2$. The platform initializes each
family rating at 1,000, so all three final ratings exceed their true platform
starts. Displayed gains are non-monotone and attenuate across chronological
thirds.

Table~\ref{tab:method-lineage} adds the historical method baselines. These
uncontrolled role-level observations are sequential development variants.
Alice's observed mean was $-3.03$ under AD ($n=5$) and $+0.43$ under SP-TP
($n=4$); the retained Bob branch means were $+0.38$ and $-1.64$ ($n=6$ each).
More strongly, the unchanged defensive negotiation rule averaged $+3.56$
under AD ($n=50$) but only $+0.24$ under RA-DC ($n=43$), consistent with
substantial temporal and matchmaking variation. Bob's observed mean was
$-1.13$ under EG-CR ($n=27$) and $+1.29$ under ELP ($n=86$); the corresponding
persuasion-seller means were $-1.58$ ($n=19$) and $+0.51$ ($n=41$).
Negotiation-buyer means remained negative ($-1.59$ and $-1.85$), while the
frozen seller means were $+1.98$ and $-1.49$. The role-isolated rollback,
Bob-recovery, and binary-pooling notebooks have executable offline designs
but no live outcomes.

ELP then targeted 150 games per family in fixed order at concurrency one.
Bargaining reached 150; persuasion stopped at 78 after a 23.19-point
drawdown, and negotiation at 20 after a 33.73-point loss. Its family
mean/median/SD were $1.27/0.19/4.36$, $0.71/-0.22/3.90$, and
$-1.69/-2.98/3.65$. The next-day snapshot ranked the entrant 95th at
1674.3 overall. These are descriptive sequential contrasts: policies were
selected from earlier runs, matchmaking and rating drift are uncontrolled,
and stopping is outcome-dependent. We report no mechanism-effect estimate
or IID interval. Appendix~\ref{app:statistics} gives full support, role,
dispersion, guard-sensitivity, and execution tables.
