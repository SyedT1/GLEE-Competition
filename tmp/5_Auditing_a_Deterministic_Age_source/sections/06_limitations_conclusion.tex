\section{Limitations and conclusion}

This post-selection, fixed-order trace has uncontrolled matchmaking, role,
configuration, opponent, rating shrinkage, and time; two families are
adaptively censored. Rating deltas are platform measures, not welfare, and
transcripts are incomplete and unlinked. Fixed text cannot test richer
dialogue. Strategic persuasion can transfer to manipulative settings despite
its calibration-heuristic label and the competition setting.

Bundled V33 evidence regenerates the final-trace tables and figure; the
1,704-game dashboard export is unavailable in this package. Mutable ratings,
missing transcripts, and counterfactual actions cannot be replayed. Legal
execution, behavior, exposure, and rating effects must be evaluated
separately; these totals do not identify mechanism effects.
